About Me

Hey, my name is Jameel Livingston. I am a UX and communication designer aspiring to create avant-garde digital and visual experiences. I have an Associate of Applied Science (AAS) degree in Graphic Design - UX from Kingsborough Community College, and I am currently continuing my educatinon at New York City College of Technology, where I am pursuing my Bachelors degree in Communication Design. I enjoy artistic expression through design experiences and take pride in producing enganging visuals.
